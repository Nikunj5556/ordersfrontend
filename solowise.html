Before you say hello.

How much commission can I earn?

The commission range is 15–40%. The specific rate, eligible amount and payment schedule depend on the agreement for the client or project. These details should be confirmed before you make a referral.

Do I need to build websites or create designs?

Your focus is finding potential clients, understanding their needs and making the connection. Solowise handles the web development and creative service delivery.

Can I apply if I’m just getting started?

Yes. Tell us about your communication skills, network and approach. The form has an option for applicants who are new to sales.

Is this a salaried job?

This page is for a commission-based sales partnership, not an offer of a fixed salary. Part-time and full-time preferences help us understand your availability; the final arrangement is discussed separately.
